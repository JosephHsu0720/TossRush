## English description（可貼入頁面）

**Six scenes. Twenty seconds. Make every throw count.**

TossRush is a fast first-person throwing game. Toss everyday objects at highlighted targets, land clean shots, and chase a new high score before the shared 20-second timer runs out.

Every hit takes you to another scene: a cozy room, a busy street, a windy basketball court, a moving carnival target, a garden mailbox, and a bar dartboard. Use the trajectory preview, account for the wind, and find your line.

**Controls**

- Move mouse — aim.
- Left click — throw. If the cursor is released, click the game to capture it first.
- Escape — release the mouse. The timer keeps running.
- R or ONE MORE ROUND — replay after the result screen.
- Use the corner icon to toggle fullscreen; release the mouse first if needed.

**Play on a desktop browser with a mouse.** Touch controls are not supported in this release. Your best score and volume preference are stored in this browser; storage restrictions or clearing browser data may remove them.

**Credits**

- Furniture, UI font and button, interface sounds: Kenney, CC0.

- Music: “Level 1” by Juhani Junkala / SubspaceAudio, CC0.
- Made with Unity, URP, Input System, uGUI, and TextMeshPro. Third-party notices are included in the release package.

## 繁體中文介紹（可選擇附在英文後）

**六種情境，共用 20 秒，每次出手都算數！**

TossRush 是第一人稱投擲小遊戲。把紙團、垃圾袋、籃球、寶特瓶、報紙與飛鏢投向標示目標，利用軌跡預覽與風向挑戰最高分。每次命中後切換情境，六種情境共用同一個 20 秒倒數。

滑鼠移動瞄準，左鍵投擲；Esc 釋放滑鼠，倒數仍會繼續。結算後按 R 或 ONE MORE ROUND 重玩。目前請使用電腦瀏覽器與滑鼠遊玩。

本作品大量使用生成式 AI 協助程式、場景、介面與 Shader 實作；由創作者負責玩法方向、試玩與修改回饋。素材、音效與音樂來源見上方致謝。
